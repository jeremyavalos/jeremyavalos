01 / THE PROBLEM

A reason to build.

New token pools produce scattered signals. Research needs a repeatable way to collect evidence and revisit what happened later.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

What I developed.

I developed an asynchronous pipeline that discovers PancakeSwap pools, checks provider and liquidity data, scores candidates and tracks subsequent outcomes.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Inside the system.

01

Discover new pools

The scanner observes PancakeSwap pool creation events on BNB Smart Chain.

02

Screen the evidence

The analysis pipeline combines liquidity, provider availability and contract-risk checks.

03

Track later outcomes

SQLite persistence connects candidates, analysis and later observations.

Engineering notes +

Scanning and outcome tracking run concurrently. Missing provider data is handled explicitly. The inspected build is observation-only: it does not sign transactions or execute trades.
