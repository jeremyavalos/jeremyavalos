01 / THE PROBLEM

A reason to build.

Choosing a private yacht should feel as effortless as the trip itself. Guests need a clear view of the fleet and a simple way to explain what they are looking for.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

What I developed.

I developed a responsive charter website and the reservation-request workflow, connecting a visual fleet catalogue with a separate API and persistent booking data.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Considered at every step.

01

Discover the fleet

Explore vessels, photographs and capacity information before choosing a trip.

02

Request a private trip

Share trip details through a guided request instead of a complicated checkout.

03

Browse in your language

The local implementation supports Spanish, English and Portuguese with remembered language preferences.

Engineering notes +

The web experience and reservation API are separate services. A request starts a conversation with the operator; the current local flow does not assume an instant payment or a confirmed booking.
